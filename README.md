# HK Job Market Visualizer

An interactive treemap visualization of Hong Kong's job market, inspired by [Andrej Karpathy's US Job Market Visualizer](https://karpathy.ai/jobs/).

Visualizes **16 industry sectors** covering **2.71M persons engaged** in surveyed establishments (~73% of Hong Kong's ~3.7M total employed population).

## Features

- **Treemap layout** — rectangle area proportional to employment size
- **4 color layers:**
  - **Employment Growth** — year-on-year change in persons engaged
  - **Vacancy Rate** — current vacancy rate by sector
  - **Hiring Demand** — year-on-year change in vacancies
  - **AI Exposure** — LLM-scored digital AI disruption exposure (0–10 scale)
- **Interactive tooltips** with detailed metrics and quarterly employment sparklines
- **Stats panels** with weighted averages, histograms, tier breakdowns, and category cross-tabulations
- **Quarterly trend data** from Q1 2023 through Q3 2025

## Data Source

All employment data is from the **Hong Kong Census and Statistics Department (C&SD)**:

- **Survey:** [Quarterly Survey of Employment and Vacancies](https://www.censtatd.gov.hk/en/scode452.html)
- **Table:** [215-16001 — Number of establishments, persons engaged, vacancies and vacancy rate analysed by industry section](https://www.censtatd.gov.hk/en/web_table.html?id=215-16001)
- **Release date:** December 2025 (latest data: September 2025)

### Coverage limitations

The establishment survey excludes:
- Civil servants (~180K workers)
- Foreign domestic helpers (~330K)
- Self-employed persons (~250K)
- Workers in establishments below the survey threshold

For total labour force statistics, see the C&SD [General Household Survey](https://www.censtatd.gov.hk/en/scode200.html).

### AI Exposure scores

The AI Exposure layer scores are generated by prompting an LLM to rate each industry sector's exposure to digital AI disruption on a 0–10 scale, calibrated to Hong Kong's specific economic structure. These are rough estimates, not rigorous predictions. A high score indicates AI is reshaping the work — not necessarily eliminating jobs. The full scoring prompt is viewable in the application.

## Tech

Single-page HTML/CSS/JS application with no dependencies. Uses canvas-based rendering with a squarified treemap layout algorithm. Data loaded from a static JSON file.
