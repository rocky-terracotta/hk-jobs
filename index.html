HK Job Market Visualizer

This tool visualizes 16 industry sectors from the HK Census & Statistics Department Quarterly Survey of Employment and Vacancies (Table 215-16001), covering 2.71M persons engaged in surveyed establishments. This represents ~73% of Hong Kong's ~3.7M total employed population — it excludes civil servants (~180K), foreign domestic helpers (~330K), the self-employed (~250K), and workers in establishments below the survey threshold. Each rectangle's area is proportional to persons engaged. Color shows the selected metric — toggle between employment growth, vacancy rate, hiring demand, and AI exposure.

Data covers quarterly snapshots from March 2023 through September 2025. Hover over any tile to see detailed metrics and a quarterly employment trend sparkline.

LLM-powered coloring: The "AI Exposure" layer uses an LLM to score each industry sector's exposure to digital AI disruption on a 0–10 scale. A high score means AI is rapidly reshaping the work — not necessarily eliminating it. Software developers and financial analysts score high because AI is transforming their work, but demand could grow as each worker becomes more productive.
